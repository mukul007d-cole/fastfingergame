🎯 Fast Click Game
A simple, fun, and addictive game where your speed and reflexes are put to the test!
Click as fast as you can within the given time limit and try to beat your high score.

🚀 Features

📚 How to Play
Click the Start button.

Click the button that appears as fast as you can before the timer runs out.

Try to beat your previous high score.

Click Restart to play again!
